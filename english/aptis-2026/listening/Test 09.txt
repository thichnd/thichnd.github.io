A. It is priced too high compared to its competitors
B. It is too similar to many existing products
C. It is not available in enough stores
Correct answer: It is too similar to many existing products
Transcript:
A company recently launched a new fitness supplement, PowerBoost, but its promotion campaign is facing some difficulties. One major issue is that the advertisements make exaggerated claims. The campaign promises that PowerBoost will dramatically increase strength and endurance in just a few days. However, many customers feel the results are not as powerful as advertised. While bold claims can attract attention, they also risk damaging trust if the product does not deliver as expected.
Another challenge is that PowerBoost is struggling to stand out in the market. Some may think the issue is pricing, but it is actually similar to its competitors. The real issue is that PowerBoost is too similar to many existing products, making it difficult for customers to see what makes it unique.
